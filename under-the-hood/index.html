How we got here

  1. 2003–2013

    Learned word representations and word2vec: words become reusable vectors. Start with embeddings.

  2. 2014–2015

    Encoder–decoder attention learns to retrieve source states while generating a translation. The fixed-vector bottleneck.

  3. 2017

    The Transformer makes attention a central operation in sequence modeling. Work through Q, K, and V.

  4. 2019–2024

    MQA, GQA, FlashAttention, and MLA address different costs of long-context inference. Compare the mechanisms.

  5. 2025–2026

    Sparse selection, compressed context, and cross-layer reuse extend the design space. Read the recent work.
